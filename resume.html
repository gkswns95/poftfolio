About Me

I am interested in practical AI modeling that can be applied in real-world industrial settings. My research has primarily focused on multi-object trajectory prediction and tracking, as well as multimodal fusion. In particular, I have concentrated on confidence estimation and deep learning-based information restoration techniques to ensure robust performance even in environments with missing data and noise. Recently, my research interests have expanded to Vision-Language-Action (VLA) modeling for robot control. I am actively building experience in intelligent robotic systems by developing VLA models for immersive human-robot interaction and implementing ROS2-based humanoid robot control systems.
